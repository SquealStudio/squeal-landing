We're not a corporation

Squeal Studio is a small team of people with an unhealthy love for video games. We don't chase funding, pitch at conferences, or build roadmaps in Notion.

We just sit down and make things. Sometimes it turns out great. Sometimes it's a mess. It's always honest work.

Current projects: Stardanie for Terraria, and our own horror game NeShumi. Both are at different stages of development.

What we're building

Terraria Mod

Stardanie

— The expansion Terraria deserves

A massive mod with new bosses, new ores, mechanics, and biomes.

  • ◆ Unique bosses with multi-phase fights
  • ◆ New ores and progression
  • ◆ Hundreds of weapons, armor pieces, and accessories
  • ◆ Custom biomes with their own ecosystems
  • ◆ Deep lore and new mechanics
In Development
Original Game

NeShumi

— An atmospheric horror game with a microphone

A horror game where you're trapped in a crumbling asylum, stalked by a blind but extremely sharp-eared creature. It can hear you through the screen.

  • ◆ Procedurally generated locations
  • ◆ Non-linear playthroughs, all down to your luck
  • ◆ Microphone input feeds the monster's hearing
  • ◆ Dark corridors, thick atmosphere, and only your flashlight
  • ◆ Randomized items and exits
